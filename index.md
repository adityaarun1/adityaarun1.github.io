## About Me

I am a Machine Learning Research Scientist at [Adobe Media and Data Sceince Research (MDSR) Laboraotory](https://adobe.mdsr.live/), India. My general areas of interest are **Computer Vision** and **Machine Learning**. Currently, my research focuses on visual reasoning models and compositional generation using diffusion models.

I recieved my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/) under the joint supervision of [Prof. C. V. Jawahar](http://faculty.iiit.ac.in/~jawahar) at  [CVIT](http://cvit.iiit.ac.in/), IIIT Hyderabad, and [Prof. M. Pawan Kumar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=BfmcfEAAAAAJ&hl=en) at [OVAL](http://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~oval), University of Oxford and Deepmind. My doctoral research focused on **weakly supervised learning** approach for visual tasks, developing probabilistic methods that enable models to learn from limited or imprecise annotations while remaining robust and generalizable. See my [PhD thesis](/assets/files/thesis/Aditya_PhD_Thesis.pdf). My Ph.D. was funded by Visvesvaraya PhD Fellowship.

Beyond my research work, the topics that intrigue me include probabilistic machine learning, uncertainty quantification, learning from limited data, and discrete & combinatorial optimization. 

I received my B.E. in Computer Science from [BIT Mesra](https://bitmesra.ac.in/) in 2013, where I was advised by [Prof. Soumya Banerjee](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ZBHxXjcAAAAJ). From 2013-2014, I worked at [IBM India](https://www.ibm.com/in-en) as Associate System Engineer. I was also the co-founder and CTO at [Drishyam.ai](https://web.archive.org/web/20200228090154/http://drishyam.ai/) [(acquired by MediaOcean)](https://www.mediaocean.com/mediaocean-acquires-drishyam-ai) from 2018-2020 and an Senior Research Analyst and Advisor at [Cognizer.ai](https://cognizer.ai/) from 2021-2022.

In my free time, I like to read books, play sports (cricket, table tennis, and running are my favorites), go on hikes, write short stories, and play guitar. Also, I was born and raised in [Patna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patna), one of the oldest continuously inhabited places in the world!
